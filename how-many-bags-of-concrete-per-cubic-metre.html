How Many Bags of Concrete Make 1 Cubic Metre?

Published 2026-10-14 · ConcreteCalc.UK

Short answer: about 83 bags of 25kg mix make one cubic metre, or around 105 bags of 20kg mix. Yields vary slightly by brand, so check the yield printed on the bag.

The sum

A 25kg bag of dry mix yields about 0.012 m³ of wet concrete. Divide your volume by that number: bags = volume ÷ 0.012. For 0.9 m³ (a 3m × 3m slab at 100mm) that is 75 bags, or 83 with 10% waste.

Why round up

Always round up to whole bags and add 5–10% for uneven ground and spillage. Running out mid-pour can leave a weak joint where fresh concrete meets concrete that has already started to set.

When bags stop making sense

Mixing 80+ bags by hand is a full day of heavy work. For anything over about 1 m³, get a ready-mix or mini-mix quote as well. Use our concrete calculator to compare both.
