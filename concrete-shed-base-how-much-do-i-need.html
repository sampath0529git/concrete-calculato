Concrete Shed Base: How Much Concrete Do You Need?

Published 2026-10-14 · ConcreteCalc.UK

A concrete base should be level, strong and slightly larger than the shed it carries. Most garden sheds sit well on a 100mm slab.

Worked example: 2.4m × 1.8m base

2.4 × 1.8 × 0.1 = 0.432 m³. Add 10% waste and you need about 0.475 m³, which is roughly 40 bags of 25kg mix.

Thickness

100mm is typical for sheds and small outbuildings. Heavier workshops or garages may need more; check the manufacturer's advice.

Sub-base and formwork

Dig out topsoil, compact 75–100mm of hardcore, and build level timber formwork. A firm sub-base helps prevent cracking and keeps your volume estimate accurate. Once you know your dimensions, enter them in our calculator for bags and cost.
